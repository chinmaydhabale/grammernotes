## 1. Verb ki Basic Definition aur Classification

### 🔍 Verb kya hota hai?
- **Definition**: Verb ek aisa shabd hai jo kisi subject ka **Action (कार्य)**, **State of Being (अवस्था/होना)**, ya **Possession (अधिकार)** darshata hai.
- **Unique Power of Verb**: Puri English grammar me **VERB akela aisa Part of Speech hai jo TENSE (समय/काल) darshata hai**! Bina Verb ke koi bhi complete sentence nahi ban sakta.
  - *Action*: Rahul **kicked** the ball. / The company **expanded** operations.
  - *State of Being*: She **is** an IAS officer. / The concept **appears** confusing.
  - *Possession*: They **have** a luxury apartment.

## 2. Finite vs. Non-Finite Verbs

```
                               ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
                               │           FINITE vs NON-FINITE         │
                               └───────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                                   │
                ┌──────────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────┐
                ▼                                                                     ▼
      【 FINITE VERBS 】                                                    【 NON-FINITE VERBS 】
      • Subject ke Number, Person aur Tense                                • Subject ya Tense badalne par
        ke anusaar BADAL JAATI HAIN.                                         KABHI NAHI BADALTI.
      • *He plays* / *They play* / *He played*                             • 3 Types: Infinitive, Gerund, Participle
```

### 🏷️ 3 Types of Non-Finite Verbs:

1. **Infinitive**:
   - **Full Infinitive**: `to + V1` (*He wants **to learn** English.*)
   - **Bare Infinitive**: `V1` without 'to' (*She made me **laugh**.*)
2. **Gerund**:
   - `V1 + ing` jo sentence me **NOUN (Subject ya Object)** ka kaam kare (* **Swimming** is a good exercise.* / *I enjoy **reading**.*)
3. **Participle**:
   - `V1 + ing` ya `V3` jo sentence me **ADJECTIVE ya Participle Clause** ka kaam kare:
     - **Present Participle (`V1 + ing`)**: Active / Ongoing state (*A **rolling** stone*, *The **collapsing** market*).
     - **Past Participle (`V3`)**: Passive / Completed state (*A **broken** window*, *The **distressed** bank*).
     - **Perfect Participle (`Having + V3`)**: Ek kaam pura hone ke baad doosra shuru hona (* **Having finished** the work, he went home.*).

## 3. Transitive vs. Intransitive Verbs

| Feature | Transitive Verb (सकर्मक क्रिया) | Intransitive Verb (अकर्मक क्रिया) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Direct Object Requirement** | Iske baad **Direct Object (What? / Whom?) aana ZAROORI** hai. | Iske baad **Direct Object KABHI NAHI** aata. |
| **Passive Voice** | Inka **Passive Voice ban sakta hai**. | Inka **Passive Voice NAHI ban sakta**. |
| **Examples** | *Raise, Lay, Cut, Buy, Bring, Give, Inform, Tell.*<br>• *He **raised** his hand.* (What? → *his hand*)<br>• *She **laid** the baby on the bed.* | *Rise, Lie, Fall, Go, Sleep, Die, Laugh, Cry.*<br>• *The sun **rises** in the east.* (No object)<br>• *He **lay** on the bed.* (No object) |

## 4. Confusing Verb Pairs (Exam Goldmine!)

Exam me in pairs par sabse zyada questions aate hain:

### 1. Lie vs. Lay:

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **LIE** | **LAY** | **LAIN** | **LYING** | लेटना / पड़े रहना / आराम करना | **Intransitive** (No Object) | *He **lay** on the grass yesterday.* / *He has **lain** in bed for hours.* |
| **LAY** | **LAID** | **LAID** | **LAYING** | रखना / बिछाना / अंडे देना | **Transitive** (Takes Object!) | *She **laid** the baby on the bed.* / *The committee has **laid** emphasis.* |

## 5. Invariable Verb Forms (Broadcast, Telecast, Cast)

Kuch verbs ke V1, V2, aur V3 forms **HAMESHA BILKUL SAME** rehte hain. Inme `-ed` lagana exam ka standard trap hai:

$$\mathbf{Broadcast → Broadcast → Broadcast\ (NOT\ broadcasted\ ❌)}$$
$$\mathbf{Telecast → Telecast → Telecast\ (NOT\ telecasted\ ❌)}$$
$$\mathbf{Cast → Cast → Cast\ (NOT\ casted\ ❌)}$$
$$\mathbf{Forecast → Forecast → Forecast\ (NOT\ forecasted\ ❌)}$$
$$\mathbf{Cost → Cost → Cost\ (NOT\ costed\ ❌)}$$
$$\mathbf{Cut,\ Put,\ Shut,\ Spread,\ Hit,\ Hurt,\ Let,\ Set → Unchanging}$$

- *Error*: *The executive board **broadcasted** the revised compliance directives.* ❌  
  → *Correction*: *The executive board **broadcast** the revised compliance directives.* ✅
- *Error*: *The live match was **telecasted** across twenty countries.* ❌  
  → *Correction*: *The live match was **telecast** across twenty countries.* ✅

## 6. Modal Auxiliaries & Marginal Modals

### 📌 General Rule:
Sabhi Modal Auxiliaries (`Can, Could, May, Might, Shall, Should, Will, Would, Must, Ought to`) ke baad hamesha **Bare Infinitive (V1 base form)** aata hai:

$$\mathbf{Modal + V1\ (Base\ Form)}$$

- *Error*: *The administration must not **underestimated** the political consequences.* ❌  
  → *Correction*: *The administration must not **underestimate** the consequences.* ✅

---

### 🔍 Marginal Modals Special Rules:

#### 1. "Used to" vs. "Be / Get Used to + V-ing":
- **Subject + used to + V1**: Past Habit (Purani aadat jo ab nahi hai) → *He **used to smoke**.* / *He **used to drive**.*
- **Subject + [is/am/are/was/were/get/got] + used to + V-ing**: Habituated to (Kisi aadat me dhal chuka hona / Aadi hona):
  - *The risk manager is completely **used to handling** market volatility.* ✅ (NOT *used to handle* ❌)
  - *The policymakers are gradually **getting used to operating** within decentralized finance.* ✅ (NOT *get used to operate* ❌)

#### 2. "Need" & "Dare":
- **As Main Verb**: Takes `to + V1` → *He **needs to disclose** the facts.* / *He **does not dare to speak**.*
- **As Modal Auxiliary (Negative / Question)**: Takes **Bare Infinitive (V1)** (Never takes `to`!):
  - *The institution **need not disclose** confidential projections.* ✅ (NOT *need not to disclose* ❌)
  - *How **dare you speak** to me like that?* ✅ (NOT *dare you to speak* ❌)

#### 3. "Had better / Had rather / Would rather / Would sooner":
In sabhi phrases ke baad hamesha **Bare Infinitive (V1)** aata hai (`to + V1` lagana strictly prohibited hai!):
- *The board of directors **had better liquidate** the distressed assets.* ✅ (NOT *had better to liquidate* ❌)
- *I **would rather resign** than compromise on ethics.* ✅ (NOT *would rather to resign* ❌)

## 7. The Bare Infinitive Master Rules

Bare Infinitive ka matlab hota hai **V1 bina "TO" ke**.

### 🌟 Verbs of Perception & Causation in ACTIVE VOICE:
Jab sentence **Active Voice** me ho, to in verbs ke baad aane wali doosri verb **Bare Infinitive (V1)** hoti hai:

> **Let, Make, Bid (Aadesh dena), Watch, Hear, See, Feel, Notice.**

- *The manager made the employees **work** beyond office hours.* ✅ (NOT *to work* ❌)
- *The principal let the participants **enter** the auditorium.* ✅ (NOT *to enter* ❌)
- *The investigators heard the chairman **disclose** classified information.* ✅ (NOT *to disclose* ❌)
- *I saw him **cross** the street.* ✅ (NOT *to cross* ❌)

---

### ⚠️ THE PASSIVE VOICE EXCEPTION (CRITICAL EXAM RULE!):
Jab yeh verbs (`Make, See, Hear, Bid`) **Passive Voice** me use hoti hain, to inke baad **`TO + V1` (Full Infinitive)** lagta hai!

$$\mathbf{Passive\ Voice\ (was/were\ made/seen/heard) ⟹ TO + V1}$$

- *Active*: *The manager made the junior analyst **revise** the report.* ✅
- *Passive*: *The junior analyst was made **to revise** the report.* ✅ (NOT *was made revise* ❌)
- *Passive*: *The compliance officer was seen **to enter** the digital facility.* ✅ (NOT *was seen enter* ❌)
- *Passive*: *The students were made **to revise** the entire syllabus.* ✅

#### 🚫 "LET" ka Passive Exception:
"LET" verb ka passive voice me 'to + V1' nahi banaya jata. Aise me 'let' ko **"allowed to"** ya **"permitted to"** se replace karte hain:
- *Error*: *The unauthorized personnel **were let to access** the server.* ❌  
  → *Correction*: *The unauthorized personnel **were allowed to access** the server.* ✅

## 8. Gerunds Deep Dive: "To + V-ing" Mandatory Phrases & Possessive Rule

### 📌 Master List of Phrases taking `TO + V-ing` (Gerund):
Aamtaur par 'to' ke baad V1 lagta hai, lekin in specific phrases me **'to' ek Preposition hai**, aur Preposition ke baad hamesha **Noun / Gerund (`V1 + ing`)** aata hai:

> **1. Look forward to**  
> **2. With a view to** (उद्देश्य से)  
> **3. Accustomed to** (आदी होना)  
> **4. Be / Get used to** (अभ्यस्त होना)  
> **5. Given to / Addicted to** (लत होना)  
> **6. Devoted to / Dedicated to**  
> **7. In addition to** (के अलावा)  
> **8. Prone to / Vulnerable to** (संवेदनशील होना)  
> **9. Averse to / Adverse to** (प्रतिकूल / अनिच्छुक)  
> **10. Object to** (आपत्ति जताना)  
> **11. Confess to** (स्वीकार करना)  
> **12. Key to** (सफलता की कुंजी)

#### Examples & Exam Corrections:
- *Error*: *With a view to **stabilize** liquid capital reserves, the company raised debt.* ❌  
  → *Correction*: *With a view to **stabilizing** liquid capital reserves...* ✅
- *Error*: *I am looking forward to **meet** the delegates.* ❌  
  → *Correction*: *I am looking forward to **meeting** the delegates.* ✅
- *Hard work is the key to **achieving** success.* ✅ (NOT *key to achieve* ❌)
- *The minister confessed to **receiving** illegal campaign funds.* ✅

---

### 👑 Possessive Case before Gerund Rule:
Jab kisi Gerund (`V-ing` acting as noun) se pehle koi Noun ya Pronoun aaye, to wo hamesha **Possessive Case** (`my, his, her, their, our, 's`) me hona chahiye:

$$\mathbf{Possessive\ Case\ (my/his/their/'s) + GERUND\ (V-ing)}$$

- *Error*: *The chairman objected to **the auditors delaying** the disclosure.* ❌  
  → *Correction*: *The chairman objected to **the auditors' delaying** the disclosure.* ✅
- *Error*: *The committee criticized **him withholding** crucial intelligence inputs.* ❌  
  → *Correction*: *The committee criticized **his withholding** crucial intelligence inputs.* ✅
- *Error*: *The manager objected to **him revealing** confidential data.* ❌  
  → *Correction*: *The manager objected to **his revealing** confidential data.* ✅
- *Error*: *We saw **her's** speaking to the auditors.* ❌  
  → *Correction*: *We saw **her** speaking to the auditors.* (Seeing action → Participle takes objective pronoun).

## 9. Participles (Present, Past, Perfect & Dangling Error)

### 1. Present Participle (`V-ing`) vs Past Participle (`V3`):
- **Present Participle (`V-ing`)**: Jo kaam chal raha hai ya jo effect create kar raha hai (*Active/Cause*):
  - *The book is **interesting**.* / *The movie was **boring**.* / *The **collapsing** credibility.*
- **Past Participle (`V3`)**: Jo kaam complete ho chuka hai ya jo effect experience kar raha hai (*Passive/Result*):
  - *The students are **interested**.* / *The audience felt **bored**.* / *The **insolvent** bank.*

---

### 2. Perfect Participle (`Having + V3`):
Jab ek action puri tarah khatam ho jaye aur uske turant baad doosra action shuru ho:
- * **Having examined** the fiscal reports carefully, the committee found several irregularities.* ✅
- * **Having worked** in volatile trading pits, the manager understood market risk.* ✅

---

### 🚫 Dangling / Misrelated Participle Error:
Participle clause ka Subject of Reference wahi hona chahiye jo main clause ka Subject hai. Agar Subject mismatch ho to sentence ka anarth nikalta hai:
- *Error*: *Walking in the garden, a snake bit Rahul.* ❌ (Iska matlab nikalta hai ki snake garden me walk kar raha tha!)  
  → *Correction*: * **While Rahul was walking in the garden**, a snake bit him.* ✅
- *Error*: *Being a rainy day, I decided to stay home.* ❌ (Iska matlab nikalta hai ki 'I' ek rainy day tha!)  
  → *Correction*: * **It being a rainy day**, I decided to stay home.* ✅

## 10. Causative Verbs (Make, Have, Get, Let, Help)

Jab Subject khud kaam nahi karta, balki kisi doosre se kaam karwata hai:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        CAUSATIVE VERBS MATRIX                          │
├───────────────┬──────────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────┤
│ Causative Verb│ Active Voice (Doer present)      │ Passive Voice       │
├───────────────┼──────────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────┤
│ **MAKE**      │ Subject + make + Person + **V1** │ was/were made + **TO + V1** │
│ **HAVE**      │ Subject + have + Person + **V1** │ have + Thing + **V3** │
│ **GET**       │ Subject + get + Person + **TO + V1** │ get + Thing + **V3** │
│ **LET**       │ Subject + let + Person + **V1**  │ was/were allowed + **TO + V1** │
│ **HELP**      │ help + Person + **(to) + V1**    │ was/were helped + **(to) + V1** │
└───────────────┴──────────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────┘
```

### Examples & Practice Corrections:
1. **MAKE (Compulsion/Force)**:
   - *Active*: *The manager made the employees **work** late.* ✅ (NOT *to work* ❌)
   - *Passive*: *The employees were made **to work** late.* ✅
2. **HAVE (Responsibility/Assignment)**:
   - *Active*: *She had the designer **renovate** her office.* ✅ (NOT *to renovate* ❌)
   - *Passive*: *We had the confidential documents **verified** before submission.* ✅ (NOT *documents verify* ❌)
3. **GET (Persuasion/Arrangement)**:
   - *Active*: *The supervisor got the workers **to complete** the assignment.* ✅ (NOT *got workers complete* ❌)
   - *Passive*: *I got my watch **repaired**.* ✅
4. **LET (Permission)**:
   - *Active*: *She let her brother **use** her laptop.* ✅
5. **HELP (Assistance)**:
   - *The AI system will help underwriters **identify** (ya **to identify**) high-risk loans.* ✅

## 11. Subjunctive Mood & Recommendation Verbs

### 🏛️ Mandative Subjunctive (Verbs of Demand / Proposal / Suggestion):
Jab sentence me koi recommendation, demand, ya advice di jaye:

> **Recommend, Suggest, Demand, Insist, Propose, Order, Mandate, Require, Urge + THAT + Subject + BASE FORM (V1)**

- **Golden Rule**: `THAT` clause ke andar aane wali verb hamesha **Base Form (V1)** me hogi — isme **`-s/-es` KABHI NAHI lagta**, chahe subject Singular (He/She/It) hi kyu na ho!

### Examples & Exam Corrections:
- *Error*: *The oversight committee recommended that the managing director **resigns** from his post.* ❌  
  → *Correction*: *The oversight committee recommended that the managing director **resign** from his post.* ✅
- *Error*: *The committee recommended that the institution **revises** its cybersecurity architecture.* ❌  
  → *Correction*: *The committee recommended that the institution **revise** its cybersecurity architecture.* ✅
- *Error*: *The doctor insisted that the patient **takes** rest.* ❌  
  → *Correction*: *The doctor insisted that the patient **take** rest.* ✅

---

### 💭 Hypothetical / Imaginary Subjunctive ('WERE'):
Kalpanik ya asambhav sthitiyon me (`If, As though, As if, Wish` ke sath) Singular subject ke sath bhi **"WERE"** lagta hai (*was* nahi):
- *Error*: *The chairman behaves as though he **was** completely unaware of the irregularity.* ❌  
  → *Correction*: *The chairman behaves as though he **were** completely unaware of the irregularity.* ✅
- *Error*: *If the administration **was** more transparent, public distrust would not rise.* ❌  
  → *Correction*: *If the administration **were** more transparent, public distrust would not rise.* ✅

## 12. Indirect Questions & Embedded Inversion Rules

### 🚫 The Embedded Question Rule:
Jab koi Wh-word (`Why, What, Where, When, How`) ya `Whether / If` kisi sentence ke **beech me** connector bankar aaye:
- **Rule**: Yeh ab Direct Question nahi raha, isliye isme **Inversion (Helping verb Subject se pehle aana) KABHI NAHI hoga**. Sentence normal Assertive structure (`Subject + Verb`) follow karega:

$$\mathbf{Connector\ (Why/How/Whether) + Subject + Helping\ Verb + Main\ Verb}$$

### Examples & Exam Corrections:
1. *The professor asked why **were the students** absent from the seminar.* ❌  
   → *The professor asked why **the students were** absent from the seminar.* ✅
2. *The committee questioned why **was the proposal** approved without review.* ❌  
   → *The committee questioned why **the proposal was** approved without review.* ✅
3. *I wonder **will the government** revise the taxation framework.* ❌  
   → *I wonder **if/whether the government will** revise the taxation framework.* ✅
4. *The board members debated whether **should the organization** expand aggressively.* ❌  
   → *The board members debated whether **the organization should** expand aggressively.* ✅
5. *The professor explained **does cognitive bias distort** rational decision-making.* ❌  
   → *The professor explained **how cognitive bias distorts** rational decision-making.* ✅

## 13. Stative Verbs (Non-Continuous Verbs)

Stative Verbs aisi kriyaayein hoti hain jo kisi **State, Sensation, Emotion, Thought, ya Possession** ko darshati hain, na ki kisi physical action ko.

### 🚫 Golden Rule: Inka use Continuous Tense (`V1 + ing`) me KABHI NAHI hota!

| Category | Stative Verbs List | Incorrect Usage ❌ | Correct Usage ✅ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Perception / Senses** | *See, Hear, Smell, Taste, Feel, Notice, Recognize* | *I am smelling something burning.* | *I **smell** something burning.* |
| **Thinking / Mental State** | *Know, Understand, Believe, Remember, Forget, Think, Doubt* | *I am knowing the answer.* | *I **know** the answer.* |
| **Feelings / Emotions** | *Love, Hate, Like, Dislike, Prefer, Want, Wish, Desire* | *She is wanting a new laptop.* | *She **wants** a new laptop.* |
| **Possession / Relation** | *Own, Possess, Belong to, Have (adhikar), Contain, Consist of, Comprise* | *This building is belonging to him.* | *This building **belongs to** him.* |
| **Appearance / State** | *Seem, Appear, Look, Resemble, Cost, Weigh* | *He is appearing confused.* | *He **appears** confused.* |

---

### 📝 Set 1: Confusing Verbs (Lie/Lay, Rise/Raise, Fall/Fell, Hang, Broadcast)

1. **Question**: *The committee had (A) / laid extensive emphasis upon (B) / institutional transparency during the summit. (C)*
   - **Error**: (E) No Error
   - **Explanation**: Lay (laid, laid) transitive verb hai aur aage object "extensive emphasis" diya hai, isliye bilkul correct hai.

2. **Question**: *The executive board (A) / broadcasted the revised compliance (B) / directives across all operational branches. (C)*
   - **Error**: Part (B)

   - **Error**: Part (B)

   - **Error**: (E) No Error

   - **Explanation**: Fell (felled, felled) transitive verb hai jiska matlab ped kaatkar girana hota hai.

## 15. Quick Revision Summary (Exam Cheat Sheet)

| Concept / Keyword | Quick Rule Formula |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lie vs Lay** | `Lie - Lay - Lain` (Intransitive - Letna) \| `Lay - Laid - Laid` (Transitive - Rakhna + Object) |
| **Rise vs Raise** | `Rise - Rose - Risen` (Intransitive - Uthna) \| `Raise - Raised - Raised` (Transitive - Uthana) |
| **Hang Forms** | `Hung - Hung` = Vastu Taangna \| `Hanged - Hanged` = Faansi Dena |
| **Broadcast / Telecast** | V1 = V2 = V3 (*Broadcast, Telecast, Cast, Forecast* - Never *-ed*) |
| **Modal + Verb** | Modals always take **Bare Infinitive (Base V1)** |
| **Used to vs Be used to** | `used to + V1` (Past habit) \| `is/are/get used to + V-ing` (Habituated to) |
| **Need / Dare as Modal** | Takes **Bare V1** (*Need not disclose*, NOT *need not to disclose*) |
| **Had better / Would rather** | Always take **Bare Infinitive (V1)** (*Had better liquidate*) |
| **Active Causative (Make/Hear/See)** | Takes **Bare Infinitive (V1)** (*Made them work*, *Heard him disclose*) |
| **Passive Causative** | Takes **TO + V1** (*Was made to revise*, *Was seen to enter*) |
| **"To + V-ing" Phrases** | *Look forward to, With a view to, Accustomed to, Prone to, Key to + V-ing* |
| **Possessive + Gerund** | Gerund se pehle hamesha **Possessive Case** (*his withholding, auditors' delaying*) |
| **Mandative Subjunctive** | *Recommend / Demand / Insist that + Subject + V1 Base Form* (Never *-s/-es*) |
| **Hypothetical Subjunctive** | *If / As though + Subject + WERE* (Even with singular subjects) |
| **Indirect Questions** | *Why / How / Whether + Subject + Verb* (No helping verb inversion in middle!) |
| **Stative Verbs** | *Know, Understand, Believe, Belong, Want* → Never in Continuous (`-ing`) |
