## 1. Noun ki Basic Definition aur Types

### 🔍 Noun kya hota hai?
- **Definition**: Noun ek **Naming Word** hota hai. Kisi bhi vyakti (Person), jagah (Place), vastu (Thing), janwar (Animal), bhavna (Feeling/Emotion), ya avastha (State/Idea) ke naam ko **Noun** kehte hain.
- **Role in a Sentence**: Sentence me Noun **"Lead Actor" / "The Hero"** hota hai. Yeh sentence me **Subject** ya **Object** banta hai.
  - *Example 1*: The **book** (Noun - Thing) is on the **table** (Noun - Place/Thing).
  - *Example 2*: **Wisdom** (Noun - Quality/Abstract) comes with **experience** (Noun - State).
  - *Example 3*: A **team** (Noun - Group) of **experts** (Noun - Person) solved the **issue** (Noun - Concept).
  - *Example 4*: A **river** (Noun - Place/Thing) flows near my **house** (Noun - Place).

---

### 🏷️ 5 Traditional Types of Noun:

```
                          ┌───────────────┐
                          │     NOUN      │
                          └───────┬───────┘
         ┌────────────┬───────────┼────────────┬─────────────┐
         ▼            ▼           ▼            ▼             ▼
    Proper Noun  Common Noun  Collective   Material Noun  Abstract Noun
                                 Noun
```

1. **Proper Noun (व्यक्तिवाचक संज्ञा)**:
   - Kisi specific person, place, ya brand ka apna naam.
   - Yeh hamesha **Capital Letter** se start hota hai.
   - *Examples*: Rahul, Sushant, Delhi, India, Samsung, Ganga.
   - *Note*: Generally, Proper Nouns singular hote hain aur inka plural nahi banta. Lekin special case me agar comparison ya counting ho: *"There are two Rohits in our batch."*

2. **Common Noun (जातिवाचक संज्ञा)**:
   - Kisi category ya class ke har member ke liye use hone wala common naam.
   - *Examples*: Boy, girl, teacher, city, country, phone, doctor, river.

3. **Collective Noun (समूहवाचक संज्ञा)**:
   - Persons, animals, ya things ke pure group/collection ko represent karne wala ek single word.
   - *Examples*: Team, jury, committee, family, flock, herd, pack, bunch.

4. **Material Noun (पदार्थवाचक संज्ञा)**:
   - Kisi matter, substance ya raw material ka naam jisse doosri cheezein banti hain.
   - Yeh hamesha **Uncountable** hota hai aur singular verb leta hai.
   - *Examples*: Gold, silver, iron, wood, cotton, water, milk, plastic.

5. **Abstract Noun (भाववाचक संज्ञा)**:
   - Aisi qualities, feelings, states ya ideas jinhe hum touch ya physically dekh nahi sakte, sirf mehsoos (feel) ya samajh sakte hain.
   - Yeh bhi hamesha **Uncountable** hota hai.
   - *Examples*: Love, honesty, wisdom, bravery, poverty, childhood, knowledge, anger, misery.

### ⚖️ The Golden Rule: Collective Noun Verb Agreement

```
                     ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
                     │            COLLECTIVE NOUN             │
                     └───────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                         │
                   ┌─────────────────────┴─────────────────────┐
                   ▼                                           ▼
         【 ACTING AS ONE (Unity) 】                 【 DIVIDED / INDIVIDUALS 】
         • Verb: Singular (is/was/has)               • Verb: Plural (are/were/have)
         • Pronoun: Singular (it / its)              • Pronoun: Plural (they / their)
```

1. **Jab group ek jut hokar (Unity) ek single unit ki tarah kaam kare**:
   - **Verb**: Singular (`is`, `was`, `has`, `V1+s/es`)
   - **Pronoun**: Singular neuter (`it`, `its`)
   - *Example*:
     - *The team **is** winning the match.* (Puri team milkar jeet rahi hai)
     - *The jury **has announced its** final verdict.* (Saare jajo ne ek mat hokar faisla sunaya)
     - *The committee **has decided** to postpone the meeting.* (Ek mat faisla)
     - *The flock of birds **was flying** in perfect formation.*

2. **Jab group ke members aapas me bate hue hon (Divided) ya alag-alag kaam kar rahe hon**:
   - **Verb**: Plural (`are`, `were`, `have`, `V1`)
   - **Pronoun**: Plural (`they`, `them`, `their`)
   - *Example*:
     - *The team **were celebrating their individual** achievements.* (Alag-alag uplabdhiyan)
     - *The jury **are divided in their** opinions regarding the verdict.* (Sabki alag-alag raye hai)
     - *The family **are quarrelling** among themselves.* (Aapas me jhagad rahe hain)
     - *The crew **were preparing** for **their respective** duties before departure.*
     - *The staff **are working** efficiently in **their respective** departments.*

### ⚠️ Special Collective / Noun-Verb Cases Mentioned in Notes:

1. **Wages**:
   - **Meaning 1: Result / Punishment of Sin/Crime (सज़ा/परिणाम)** → **Singular Verb**
     - *The wages of sin/crime **is** misery/death.* ✅
   - **Meaning 2: Salary / Daily Pay (मजदूरी/वेतन)** → **Plural Verb**
     - *The wages of the workers **are** distributed.* ✅

2. **Mathematics / Subject Names**:
   - **Meaning 1: Academic Subject (विषय)** → **Singular Verb**
     - *Mathematics **is** essential for accounting.* ✅
   - **Meaning 2: Calculations / Figures (गणना/हिसाब-किताब)** → **Plural Verb**
     - *The mathematics of this budget **are** incorrect.* ✅
     - *The mathematics of the income and salaries **are** convoluted.* ✅

3. **Police**:
   - Police hamesha **Plural** treat hota hai aur **Plural Verb** leta hai!
     - *The police **are** investigating the cause of the sudden explosion.* (NOT *is* ❌)

## 3. Countable vs. Uncountable Nouns

### 📊 Master Comparison Table:

| Feature / Lakshan | Countable Noun (Ganne yogya) | Uncountable Noun (Agadniya / Mass / Concept) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | Jinhe 1, 2, 3 karke count kiya ja sakta hai | Jinhe alag se gina nahi ja sakta, sirf measure/weigh karte hain |
| **Examples** | Book, pen, man, spoon, building, elephant | Water, coffee, air, salt, sugar, love, advice, money |
| **Articles (a / an)** | Uses **a / an** (*a book, an apple*) | **NO a / an** directly (*an advice* ❌ → *a piece of advice* ✅) |
| **Plural Form** | Has plural forms (*books, men, pens*) | **NO plural form** (*informations* ❌, *furnitures* ❌) |
| **Question Word** | Ask **"How many...?"** | Ask **"How much...?"** |
| **Small Amount** | Use **a few / few / the few** | Use **a little / little / the little** |
| **Large Amount** | Use **many** or **a lot of** | Use **much** or **a lot of** |
| **Neutral Quantity** | Use **some** (*some books*) | Use **some** (*some water*) |
| **Comparison** | Use **fewer** (*fewer students*) | Use **less** (*less water, less sugar*) |
| **Measurement** | Counted directly by numbers (*1, 2, 3...*) | Uses units of measurement (*glass, cup, kg, litre, loaf, piece*) |

---

### 🚫 Complete List of Uncountable Nouns (Jo Exam me baar-baar aate hain):
Yeh nouns hamesha **Singular** hote hain, inke aage `a/an` nahi lagta aur inme `-s/-es` jodkar plural nahi banaya ja sakta:

> **Information, Advice, Furniture, Luggage, Baggage, News, Scenery, Poetry, Progress, Research, Weather, Money, Bread, Rice, Milk, Water, Sugar, Flour, Salt, Hair (as a whole mass), Knowledge, Equipment, Work, Traffic, Evidence, Hardware, Machinery, Cutlery, Jewellery.**

#### 💡 Uncountable Nouns ko Countable kaise banayein? (Using Units/Partitives):
- **Advice** → *a piece of advice* / *two pieces of advice* (NOT *advices* ❌)
- **Information** → *a piece of information* / *many pieces of information* (NOT *informations* ❌)
- **Furniture** → *an item / a piece of furniture* (NOT *furnitures* ❌)
- **Luggage / Baggage** → *an article / a piece of luggage* (NOT *luggages* ❌)
- **Bread** → *a loaf of bread* / *two slices of bread* (NOT *two breads* ❌)
- **Equipment** → *a piece of equipment* / *several pieces of equipment* (NOT *equipments* ❌)
- **Work** → *a piece of work* / *urgent pieces of work* (NOT *works* jab kaam ka sense ho ❌)
- **Research** → *a piece of research* / *conducted research* (NOT *a research* ❌)

| **Experience** | Knowledge gained over time (अनूभव - Uncountable) | *Wisdom comes with experience.* | **Experiences** | Specific events / Incidents (घटनाएं/वाकये - Countable) | *He shared many valuable experiences.* |

## 5. Plural-Only Nouns

Kuch nouns **hamesha Plural** form me hi rehte hain, inka singular nahi banta, aur yeh hamesha **Plural Verb** lete hain:

### Categories of Plural-Only Nouns:

1. **Two-part Tools, Instruments & Clothing (दो बराबर हिस्सों वाली वस्तुएं)**:
   - *Trousers, Pants, Jeans, Scissors, Spectacles, Glasses, Binoculars, Tongs, Pliers, Pincers, Shorts, Tights.*
   - **Verb**: Plural → *My trousers **are** too tight.* / *The scissors **are** lying on the table.*
   - ⚠️ **"A pair of" Exception**: Agar inke pehle `a pair of` lag jaye, to Subject `pair` (Singular) ban jata hai:
     - *A pair of scissors **is** missing from the toolkit.* ✅
     - *Two pairs of trousers **were** purchased.* ✅

2. **Group of People / Living Beings (दिखने में Singular, लेकिन हमेशा Plural)**:
   - *Police, Cattle, Clergy (पादरी वर्ग), Gentry (कुलीन वर्ग), Poultry (मुर्गी पालन/पक्षी), People (लोग), Vermin (कीड़े-मकोड़े/हानिकारक जीव), Staff (कर्मचारी वर्ग).*
   - *Examples*:
     - *The police **have** arrested the suspect.* (NOT *has* ❌)
     - *The cattle **are** grazing in the field.* (NOT *cattles* ❌, NOT *is* ❌)
     - *The gentry of the town **were** invited to the charity gala.* (NOT *was* ❌)
     - *The vermin **have** damaged the grain.* (NOT *has* ❌)

3. **Financial / Asset / Other Nouns**:
   - *Savings, Earnings, Belongings, Valuables, Remains, Alms (भीख/दान), Wages (तनख्वाह), Goods (सामान), Surroundings, Assets, Proceeds, Riches.*
   - *Examples*:
     - *Her earnings **are** not enough to support the family.* (NOT *is* ❌)
     - *His savings **have** been exhausted.* (NOT *has* ❌)
     - *His belongings **were** stolen.* (NOT *was* ❌)
     - *Alms **were** given to the beggar.* (NOT *an alms* ❌, NOT *was* ❌)

## 6. Zero-Plural Nouns (Unchanging Plurals)

In nouns ka **Singular aur Plural form EXACTLY SAME** hota hai. Inme kabhi bhi `-s` ya `-es` nahi lagaya jata:

| Category | Words (Singular = Plural) | Correct Usage Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Animals** | **Sheep, Deer, Fish, Bison, Moose, Swine** | • *One sheep **is** grazing.* / *Ten sheep **are** grazing.* (NOT *sheeps* ❌)<br>• *I saw a deer.* / *All the deer **were** grazing.* (NOT *deers* ❌)<br>• *A school of fish.* (Note: *Fishes* tab use hota hai jab different **species** ki baat ho) |
| **Vehicles / Aviation** | **Aircraft, Spacecraft, Hovercraft** | • *One aircraft **has** landed.*<br>• *All three aircraft **were** landed safely.* (NOT *aircrafts* ❌) |
| **Family / Biology** | **Offspring (संतान), Species (प्रजाति), Series (श्रृंखला)** | • *He divided his fortune among his offspring.* (NOT *offsprings* ❌)<br>• *This species **is** rare.* / *These species **are** endangered.*<br>• *A test series **is** going on.* / *Two series **have** been played.* |
| **Measurements** | **Score (=20), Gross (=144)** | • *Three score years and ten.* |
| **Legal / Professional** | **Counsel (वकील/कानूनी सलाहकार)** | • *The counsel **are** ready for trial.* |

## 7. Noun + Preposition + Noun Rule

Jab do **SAME (ek jaise) Nouns** kisi **Preposition** se jude hote hain:

$$\mathbf{Noun\ (Singular) + Preposition + Noun\ (Singular) ⟹ Verb\ (Singular)}$$

- **Rule**: Dono nouns hamesha **Singular** honge aur aage aane wali verb bhi hamesha **Singular** hogi!

### Examples & Common Errors:
1. *The villagers went from **doors to doors**.* ❌  
   → *The villagers went from **door to door**.* ✅
2. * **Cities after cities were** conquered by the great emperor.* ❌  
   → * **City after city was** conquered by the great emperor.* ✅
3. * **Rows upon rows** of pink marble **looks** beautiful.* ❌  
   → * **Row upon row** of pink marble **looks** beautiful.* ✅
4. * **Pages after pages** of the holy book **were** read.* ❌  
   → * **Page after page** of the holy book **was** read.* ✅
5. * **Woman after woman have** come to speak against the new law.* ❌  
   → * **Woman after woman has** come to speak against the new law.* ✅
6. Other examples: *Day by day, Word for word, Match after match, Wave upon wave.*

## 8. Compound Nouns aur unka Plural Formation

### 🔍 Compound Noun kya hota hai?
Jab do ya do se zyada words milkar ek single noun ka kaam karte hain, to use **Compound Noun** kehte hain (e.g., *Brother-in-law, Passer-by*).

### 🎯 Plural banane ka niyam:
Compound Noun ko plural banate waqt `-s/-es` hamesha **Main / Root / Principal word** me lagta hai, preposition ya suffix me nahi!

| Singular Compound Noun | Plural Form | Root / Main Word |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Commander-in-chief** | **Commanders-in-chief** (NOT *commander-in-chiefs* ❌) | Commander |
| **Passer-by** (राही/गुजरने वाला) | **Passers-by** (NOT *passer-bys* ❌) | Passer |
| **Looker-on** (तमाशबीन/दर्शक) | **Lookers-on** (NOT *looker-ons* ❌) | Looker |
| **Brother-in-law** | **Brothers-in-law** (NOT *brother-in-laws* ❌) | Brother |
| **Mother-in-law** | **Mothers-in-law** | Mother |
| **Father-in-law** | **Fathers-in-law** | Father |
| **Sister-in-law** | **Sisters-in-law** | Sister |
| **Step-daughter** | **Step-daughters** | Daughter |
| **Step-son** | **Step-sons** | Son |
| **Maid-servant** | **Maid-servants** | Servant |
| **Member of Parliament** | **Members of Parliament** | Member |
| **Attorney-General** | **Attorneys-General** (NOT *attorney-generals* ❌) | Attorney |
| **Man-servant** | **Men-servants** (*Dono words plural bante hain*) | Man + Servant |
| **Woman-doctor** | **Women-doctors** (*Dono words plural bante hain*) | Woman + Doctor |
| **Lord-Justice** | **Lords-Justices** (*Dono words plural bante hain*) | Lord + Justice |
| **Spoonful** (चम्मच भर) | **Spoonfuls** (NOT *spoonsful* ❌) | Spoonful |
| **Cupful** (कप भर) | **Cupfuls** (NOT *cupsful* ❌) | Cupful |
| **Handful** (मुट्ठी भर) | **Handfuls** (NOT *handsful* ❌) | Handful |

## 9. Nouns & Numbers: Quantifiers, Units aur Hyphenated Adjectives

Units: *Hundred, Thousand, Million, Billion, Dozen, Score, Year, Rupee, Dollar.*

### Case 1: Definite Numeral (Nischit Sankhya di ho: 1, 2, 3, 5, 7...)
$$\mathbf{Definite\ Number + Unit\ (Singular) + Plural\ Noun}$$
- **Rule**: Unit me `-s` **nahi** lagega aur unit ke baad `of` **nahi** lagega!
  - *She bought **five dozen apples**.* ✅ (NOT *five dozens apples* ❌, NOT *five dozens of apples* ❌)
  - *The company sold **two million units**.* ✅ (NOT *two millions units* ❌)
  - *Seven **million people** attended the ceremony.* ✅ (NOT *seven millions of people* ❌)
  - *He gave me **three hundred rupees**.* ✅

---

### Case 2: Indefinite Numeral (Anischit Sankhya / Vague quantity ho: "Hazaron", "Lakhon")
$$\mathbf{Unit\ (Plural) + of + Plural\ Noun}$$
- **Rule**: Unit me `-s` **lagega** aur aage `of` **zaroor aayega**!
  - *She bought **dozens of bananas**.* ✅ (NOT *dozen of bananas* ❌)
  - * **Hundreds of students** were present.* ✅
  - * **Thousands of migrants** are returning.* ✅
  - * **Millions of people** attended.* ✅

---

### Case 3: Hyphenated / Compound Adjective Rule (Numerical + Noun acting as Adjective)
Jab koi Number + Noun kisi doosre Noun se pehle aakar uski visheshta (Adjective ka kaam) bataye:
- **Rule**: Adjective ka kabhi plural nahi banta, isliye beech ka Noun **hamesha Singular** rahega!

| Adjective Position (Singular Noun) | Normal Noun / Predicate Position (Plural Noun) |
| :--- | :--- |
| *He is a **twenty-year-old boy**.* ✅ (boy ke aage adjective hai) | *He is **twenty years old**.* ✅ (normal noun phrase) |
| *A **twenty-page report**.* ✅ (NOT *twenty-pages report* ❌) | *The report has **twenty pages**.* ✅ |
| *A **three-hundred-page study**.* ✅ (NOT *three-hundred-pages study* ❌) | *The study has **three hundred pages**.* ✅ |
| *A **five-hundred-dollar note** was lying on the floor.* ✅ | *I have **five hundred dollars**.* ✅ |
| *A **ten-mile walk**.* ✅ | *We walked **ten miles**.* ✅ |
| *A **five-star hotel**.* ✅ (NOT *five-stars hotel* ❌) | *This hotel has **five stars**.* ✅ |
| *A **three-man committee**.* ✅ | *The committee has **three men**.* ✅ |

## 10. Fractions & Percentage Rules with Nouns

Fractions (*one-third of, two-thirds of, three-fourths of, half of, percentage of*):
- **Golden Rule**: Verb fraction par depend nahi karti, balki **'of' ke baad aane wale Noun** par depend karti hai!

```
                               ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
                               │       FRACTION / PERCENTAGE + OF       │
                               └───────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                                   │
                ┌──────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────┐
                ▼                                  ▼                                  ▼
      【 + Uncountable Noun 】           【 + Plural Countable Noun 】        【 + Single Object (Part) 】
      • Verb: SINGULAR                   • Verb: PLURAL                      • Verb: SINGULAR
```

1. **Fraction / % + Uncountable Noun ⟹ Singular Verb**:
   - *Three-fourths of the **work is** complete.* (work = uncountable)
   - *20% of the **water has** evaporated.* (water = uncountable)
   - *90% of the **information was** false.* (information = uncountable)
   - *Three-fourths of the **business is** left for the youngest son.*

2. **Fraction / % + Plural Countable Noun ⟹ Plural Verb**:
   - *Two-thirds of the **students are** absent.* (students = plural countable)
   - *50% of the **apples were** rotten.* (apples = plural countable)
   - *More than half of the **workers are** on strike today.* (workers = plural countable)
   - *40% of the **students are** eligible for the scholarship.*

3. **Fraction / % + Singular Countable Noun (Kisi ek cheez ka hissa) ⟹ Singular Verb**:
   - *Half of the **cake was** eaten.* (Ek hi cake ka aadha hissa)
   - *One-third of the **pillar has** rotted away.* (Ek hi pillar ka ek-tihai hissa)
   - *Three-fourths of the **apple crop was** lost.* (Puri crop ek single harvest hai)

## 11. Plural Nouns as a Single Unit (Distance, Time, Money, Weight)

Jab plural nouns (jaise *Ten miles, Fifty thousand rupees, Five years, Three hours*) sentence me aayein:

### Condition A: Single Unitary Quantity / Aggregate Amount ⟹ Singular Verb
Jab puri rashi, doori, ya samay ko **ek single unit / lump sum** ki tarah dekha jaye:
- * **Ten kilometres is** a long distance to walk without rest.* ✅
- * **Three miles is** too far to walk.* ✅
- * **Fifty thousand rupees has been deposited** in my account.* ✅
- * **Ten dollars is** a high price to pay.* ✅
- * **Five years is** the maximum sentence for that offence.* ✅
- * **Three hours is** sufficient to complete the examination.* ✅
- * **Six months is** a short duration for a research project.* ✅
- * **One hundred metres is** a sprinting event in athletics.* ✅
- * **Thirty rupees was** all he had in his pocket.* ✅

### Condition B: Divided / Separate Entities / Separate Action ⟹ Plural Verb
Jab alag-alag notes, alag-alag ghante, ya bante hue paison ki baat ho:
- *Ten dollars (i.e., dollar notes/bills) **were scattered** on the floor.* ✅
- *Four hours **were spent** solving the problem yesterday.* ✅ (Ghante alag-alag beete)
- *Twenty thousand rupees that were distributed among the workers **have been accounted for**.* ✅
- *Ten years **have passed** since the policy was implemented.* ✅ (Saal ek-ek karke beete)

## 14. "One of the..." Structure & Exceptions

### Rule 1: Basic Structure
$$\mathbf{One\ of\ the + Plural\ Noun + Singular\ Verb}$$
- Chunav (Selection) hamesha **zyada me se ek** ka hota hai, isliye Noun hamesha Plural hoga, lekin baat sirf **"One"** ki ho rahi hai isliye Verb hamesha Singular hogi!
  - *One of the **boys has** submitted the homework late.* ✅ (NOT *boys have* ❌, NOT *boy has* ❌)
  - *One of the **players is** missing from the team.* ✅
  - *One of the **books has been** misplaced.* ✅
  - *One of the **teachers has suggested** a new topic.* ✅
  - *One of the **animals has escaped** from the zoo.* ✅
  - *One of the **policemen was** injured during the protest.* ✅
  - *One of the **options seems** better than the others.* ✅

---

### Rule 2: Relative Pronoun Exception (`who / which / that`)
$$\mathbf{One\ of\ the + Plural\ Noun + Relative\ Pronoun\ (who/which/that) + PLURAL\ VERB}$$
- Jab Plural Noun ke baad relative pronoun aa jaye, to aage aane wali verb apne **Antecedent (Plural Noun)** ke anusaar lagti hai, isliye verb **Plural** hogi!
  - *She is one of the **best athletes that have** ever represented this country.* ✅ (Antecedent = *athletes* → *have*, NOT *has* ❌)
  - *He is one of those **authors who have** won international acclaim.* ✅

---

### Rule 3: "The Only One of..." Exception
$$\mathbf{The\ only\ one\ of\ the + Plural\ Noun + Relative\ Pronoun + SINGULAR\ VERB}$$
- Jab sentence me **"The only one"** lag jaye, to focus sirf us ek par aa jata hai:
  - *He is **the only one of the players who has** performed consistently.* ✅

## 15. Subject-Verb Agreement with Conjunctions & Prepositional Connectors

### 🅰️ First Subject Rule (Subject 1 ke hisaab se Verb):
Jab do subjects in connectors se jude hon:
> **As well as, Along with, Together with, Coupled with, In addition to, With, Accompanied by, Like, Unlike, Besides, Except, Rather than, And not.**

$$\mathbf{Subject\ 1 + Connector + Subject\ 2 ⟹ Verb\ according\ to\ Subject\ 1}$$

- *Examples*:
  - *The **manager** (S1 - Sing), along with his team members (S2 - Plur), **is planning** a new product.* ✅ (NOT *are* ❌)
  - *The **quality** of products (S1 - Sing), as well as their packaging, **is impressive**.* ✅ (NOT *are* ❌)
  - *The **teacher** (S1 - Sing) as well as the students **was sad**.* ✅ (NOT *were* ❌)
  - *The **CEO** (S1 - Sing), coupled with his advisors, **has decided** to revise the strategy.* ✅ (NOT *have* ❌)
  - *The **players** (S1 - Plur), together with their coach, **are determined** to win.* ✅ (NOT *is* ❌)
  - *The **house** (S1 - Sing), together with all its furniture, **is being sold**.* ✅
  - *The **clergy** (S1 - Plur), along with the steno, **are moving** towards the hill.* ✅

---

### 🅱️ Nearest Subject Rule (Subject 2 / Paas wale Subject ke hisaab se Verb):
Jab do subjects in correlative conjunctions se jude hon:
> **Either...or, Neither...nor, Not only...but also, Or, Nor.**

$$\mathbf{Subject\ 1 + [Neither/Either/Not\ only]...[nor/or/but\ also] + Subject\ 2 ⟹ Verb\ according\ to\ Subject\ 2}$$

- *Examples*:
  - *Neither the teachers (S1) nor the **principal** (S2 - Sing) **has approved** the proposal.* ✅ (NOT *have* ❌)
  - *Either the manager (S1) or his **subordinates** (S2 - Plur) **are responsible**.* ✅ (NOT *is* ❌)
  - *Not only the CEO (S1) but also the **board members** (S2 - Plur) **were present**.* ✅ (NOT *was* ❌)
  - *Neither the children (S1) nor their **mother** (S2 - Sing) **enjoys** horror movies.* ✅ (NOT *enjoy* ❌)
  - *Neither my sister (S1) nor **I** (S2) **am responsible** for the confusion.* ✅ (NOT *is* ❌)
  - *Either you (S1) or **he** (S2) **has to take** the responsibility.* ✅ (NOT *have* ❌)

#### ⚖️ Parallelism Rule with "Not only...but also":
"Not only" aur "but also" sentence me same grammatical parts ke aage lagne chahiye (Verb ke aage Verb, Noun ke aage Noun, Preposition ke aage Preposition):
- *Error*: *She **not only failed** in physics but also in science.* ❌
- *Correction*: *She failed **not only in physics but also in science**.* ✅
- *Error*: *He **not only completed** the project on time but also with accuracy.* ❌
- *Correction*: *He completed the project **not only on time but also with accuracy**.* ✅
- *Error*: *The company **not only reduced** costs but also **increasing** its efficiency.* ❌
- *Correction*: *The company **not only reduced** costs but also **increased** its efficiency.* ✅

## 16. Article + Noun Combinations (Single Person vs Multiple Persons)

Jab do ya do se zyada designations/roles `and` se jude hon:

### 1. Article / Possessive sirf Pehle Noun ke aage ho ⟹ Ek hi Vyakti (Singular Verb)
- * **The director, producer and actor has arrived** at the venue.* ✅ (Ek hi insaan teenon roles me hai → Singular Verb)
- * **A teacher, mentor and guide plays** an important role.* ✅ (Singular Verb)
- *He is **a poet, philosopher and thinker** admired across the world.* ✅

### 2. Article / Possessive Har Noun ke aage alag-alag ho ⟹ Alag-alag Vyakti (Plural Verb)
- * **The principal and the teacher are present** in the classroom.* ✅ (Do alag-alag log hain → Plural Verb)
- * **A scientist, an innovator and a visionary were awarded** for their contributions.* ✅ (Teen alag log)
- *We invited **a singer, a dancer and a choreographer** to perform.* ✅

## 17. Adjective as Noun ("The + Adjective" Rule)

Jab kisi Adjective ke pehle definite article **"The"** lagaya jata hai, to wo puri community / group of people ko represent karne wala **Plural Common Noun** ban jata hai:

$$\mathbf{The + Adjective = Plural\ Noun ⟹ Plural\ Verb}$$

- **Rules**:
  1. Inke aage **Plural Verb** lagti hai (`are`, `were`, `have`, `V1`).
  2. Inme kabhi bhi alag se `-s` ya `-es` **nahi** lagaya jata (*The poors* ❌ → *The poor* ✅).

| Adjective with 'The' | Meaning | Correct Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The poor** | All poor people (गरीब लोग) | *The poor in rural areas **have been neglected**.* (NOT *has been* ❌) |
| **The rich / The wealthy** | All wealthy people (अमीर लोग) | *The wealthy **are expected** to contribute more.* (NOT *is* ❌) |
| **The elderly** | All old/elderly people (बुजुर्ग लोग) | *The elderly **require** special attention.* (NOT *requires* ❌) |
| **The unemployed** | All unemployed youth (बेरोजगार लोग) | *The unemployed **are demanding** policy reforms.* (NOT *is* ❌) |
| **The underprivileged** | All deprived people (वंचित लोग) | *The underprivileged **are expected** to benefit.* (NOT *is* ❌) |
| **The marginalized** | All marginalized people (हाशिए पर खड़े लोग) | *The marginalized **are facing** discrimination.* (NOT *is* ❌) |
| **The resilient** | All resilient people (धैर्यवान/दृढ़ लोग) | *In times of crisis, the resilient **are** often the ones to survive.* |

## 18. Nouns and Cases: Possessive Case ('s aur of) Master Rules

### 🔍 3 Cases of Nouns:
1. **Subjective / Nominative Case**: Jab Noun sentence me Verb ka Subject ban kar aaye (* **Rahul** kicked the ball* / *The **sun** shines*).
2. **Objective / Accusative Case**: Jab Noun Verb ya Preposition ka Object bane (*They taught **Sushant***).
3. **Possessive / Genitive Case**: Jab Noun ka kisi doosre Noun par adhikar (Ownership/Possession) ya relation dikhana ho (`'s` ya `of`).

---

### 👑 Possessive Case ke Comprehensive Niyam:

#### Niyam 1: Living vs. Non-Living
- **Living Beings** ke sath `'s` lagta hai: *Ram's car, Rohit's book, teacher's desk.*
- **Non-Living Objects** ke sath `'s` nahi lagta, `of` ka use hota hai:
  - *The table's leg* ❌ → *The **leg of the table*** ✅
  - *The house's roof* ❌ → *The **roof of the house*** ✅

#### 🌟 Non-Living ke sath `'s` lagne ke 5 Important Exceptions:
1. **Time Expressions (समय)**:
   - *A day's work* ✅, *Two weeks' vacation* ✅, *A month's leave* ✅, *Today's newspaper* ✅.
2. **Distance / Weight / Value (दूरी / वजन / मूल्य)**:
   - *A mile's walk / distance* ✅, *A stone's throw* ✅, *A pound's weight* ✅, *A rupee's worth* ✅.
3. **Personification / Heavenly Bodies (मानवीकरण / आकाशीय पिंड)**:
   - *Nature's beauty* ✅, *Nature's love* ✅, *Earth's gravity* ✅, *The Sun's rays* ✅, *The Moon's beauty* ✅, *Death's icy hand* ✅, *Fortune's favorite* ✅.
4. **Countries / Cities / Organizations (देश / संस्थाएं / समूह)**:
   - *India's economy* ✅, *The company's policy* ✅, *The government's decision* ✅, *The team's performance* ✅.
5. **Idiomatic Expressions (मुहावरे)**:
   - *At one's wit's end* ✅, *At an arm's length* ✅, *For mercy's sake* ✅.

---

#### Niyam 2: Plural Nouns ending with 'S' (Hissing Sound Rule)
- Agar koi Plural Noun pehle se `-s` par end ho raha hai, to sirf **Apostrophe (`'`)** lagega, dobara `s` nahi lagate (hissing sound avoid karne ke liye):
  - *This is a **boys' hostel**.* ✅ (NOT *boys's hostel* ❌)
  - *The **teachers' meeting** was cancelled.* ✅ (NOT *teachers's meeting* ❌)
  - *A **girls' school**.* ✅ (NOT *girls's school* ❌)
- **Note**: Jo Plural nouns `-s` par end nahi hote, unme normal `'s` lagega:
  - *Children's park* ✅, *Women's club* ✅, *Men's wear* ✅.

---

#### Niyam 3: Double Apostrophe Error (लगातार दो 's नहीं आ सकते)
English grammar me lagataar do apostrophes (`'s`) lagana strictly prohibited hai. Aise me structure ko `of` use karke rewrite karte hain:
- *My brother's friend's car was stolen.* ❌  
  → * **The car of my brother's friend** was stolen.* ✅
- *Sheela's mother's advice helped me.* ❌  
  → * **The advice of Sheela's mother** helped me.* ✅

---

#### Niyam 4: Compound Nouns me Possessive Case
Compound Nouns me plural `-s` main word par lagta hai, lekin possessive `'s` hamesha **LAST WORD** par lagta hai:
- *My brother's-in-law house is nearby.* ❌  
  → *My **brother-in-law's house** is nearby.* ✅
- *The **Commander-in-chief's order** was final.* ✅
- *The **Government of India's policy**.* ✅

---

#### Niyam 5: Indefinite Pronoun + "Else"
- *Somebody's car has met with an accident.* ✅
- Lekin agar `else` laga ho, to apostrophe `'s` hamesha **`else` ke upar shift** ho jata hai:
  - *Somebody's else car* ❌ → * **Somebody else's car** * ✅
  - *Nobody else's fault* ✅
  - *Anyone else's advice* ✅

---

#### Niyam 6: Joint Possession vs. Separate Possession
1. **Joint Possession (Sanjha Adhikar / Ek hi cheez dono ki)**:
   - Apostrophe `'s` sirf **Last Noun** par lagega → Noun & Verb singular/plural context ke anusaar:
     - * **Ram and Sita's marriage** * ✅ (Dono ki ek hi shaadi)
     - * **Mummy and Papa's room** * ✅ (Ek hi kamra dono ka)
     - * **Kapil and Sunil's comedy act** * ✅ (Ek sath kiya gaya act)
     - * **Amit and Sunil's friend is coming.** * ✅ (Dono ka ek common dost)

2. **Separate Possession (Alag-alag Adhikar / Individual Possession)**:
   - Apostrophe `'s` **Dono Nouns** par alag-alag lagega → Noun & Verb hamesha **Plural** honge:
     - * **Sachin's and Kohli's batting styles** are different.* ✅ (Dono ki alag-alag styles)
     - * **The husband's and the wife's signatures** are required.* ✅ (Dono ke alag-alag dastakhat)
     - * **Amit's and Sunil's friends are coming.** * ✅ (Amit ke dost aur Sunil ke dost)
     - * **Frank Lloyd Wright's and Le Corbusier's unique design philosophies**.* ✅

---

#### Niyam 7: Gerund (V-ing) se pehle Noun / Pronoun ka Possessive Case
Jab kisi Gerund (`Verb + ing` jo Noun ka kaam kar raha ho) se pehle koi Noun ya Pronoun aaye, to wo hamesha **Possessive Case** (`'s` ya `my, his, their, our`) me hona chahiye:
- *The board was surprised at **the doctor arriving** so early.* ❌  
  → *The board was surprised at **the doctor's arriving** so early.* ✅
- *The possibility of **the team winning** the championship is slim.* ❌  
  → *The possibility of **the team's winning** the championship is slim.* ✅
- *The administration was concerned about **the doctor performing** the surgery.* ❌  
  → *The administration was concerned about **the doctor's performing** the surgery.* ✅
- *I don't mind **him coming** late.* ❌ → *I don't mind **his coming** late.* ✅

## 21. Miscellaneous Quantifiers: "Amount of" vs "Number of" & "Many a"

### 1. "Amount of" vs. "Number of":
- **Amount of / Quantity of**: Hamesha **Uncountable Nouns** ke sath use hota hai.
  - *The **amount of money / work / water**.* ✅
  - *Error*: *The **amount of cars** on the highway has decreased.* ❌  
    → *Correction*: *The **number of cars** on the highway has decreased.* ✅ (Cars = Countable)
- **Number of**: Hamesha **Countable Nouns** ke sath use hota hai.

---

### 2. "Many a / Many an" Structure:
$$\mathbf{Many\ a / Many\ an + Singular\ Countable\ Noun + Singular\ Verb}$$
- Meaning 'Many' (kai/anek) jaisa hota hai, lekin structure strictly **Singular** hota hai!
  - *Error*: * **Many a students have failed** to understand the concept.* ❌  
    → *Correction*: * **Many a student has failed** to understand the concept.* ✅
  - *Error*: * **Many a times** the company's strategies have failed.* ❌  
    → *Correction*: * **Many a time** the company's strategies have failed.* ✅
  - *Error*: * **Many a analyst** in the firm **have raised** concerns.* ❌  
    → *Correction*: * **Many an analyst** in the firm **has raised** concerns.* ✅

## 🎯 Quick Revision Summary (Exam Cheat Sheet)

| Concept / Keyword | Quick Rule Formula |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Collective Noun** | Unity → Singular Verb + `its` \| Divided → Plural Verb + `their` |
| **Uncountable Nouns** | No `a/an`, No `-s/-es`, Always Singular Verb (*Advice, Furniture, Luggage, Equipment, Information, Bread*) |
| **Plural-Only Nouns** | Always Plural Verb (*Police, Cattle, Gentry, Scissors, Trousers, Earnings, Savings*) |
| **Zero-Plural Nouns** | Singular = Plural (*Sheep, Deer, Fish, Aircraft, Offspring, Species, Series*) |
| **Noun + Prep + Noun** | Singular Noun + Prep + Singular Noun + Singular Verb (*Door to door, Page after page was*) |
| **Compound Plurals** | `-s` on Root Word (*Commanders-in-chief, Passers-by, Brothers-in-law, Women-doctors*) |
| **Definite Number** | Digit + Unit (Singular) + Plural Noun (*Five dozen apples, Two million units*) |
| **Indefinite Number** | Unit (Plural) + of + Plural Noun (*Dozens of apples, Millions of people*) |
| **Hyphenated Adjective** | Number + Singular Noun (*A twenty-year-old boy, a twenty-page report*) |
| **Fractions / %** | Verb depends strictly on the Noun following `of` |
| **Units as Single Entity** | Lump sum / Distance / Duration → Singular Verb (*Ten miles is, 50,000 rupees is*) |
| **A vs The Number of** | `A number of` → Plural Verb \| `The number of` → Singular Verb |
| **A great number vs deal** | `A great number of` + Plural Countable \| `A great deal of` + Uncountable |
| **One of the...** | `One of the` + Plural Noun + Singular Verb (*Exception*: Relative pronoun `who/that` → Plural Verb) |
| **Along with / As well as** | Verb agrees with **Subject 1** |
| **Either..or / Neither..nor** | Verb agrees with **Nearest Subject (Subject 2)** |
| **The + Adjective** | Acts as Plural Common Noun → Plural Verb (*The poor are, The elderly require*) |
| **Double Apostrophe** | Strictly Prohibited → Use `of` (*The car of my brother's friend*) |
| **Compound Possessive** | `'s` goes on the **Last Word** (*Brother-in-law's house*) |
| **Gerund + Possessive** | Noun/Pronoun before Gerund must be in Possessive Case (*the doctor's arriving, the team's winning*) |
| **Many a / an** | `Many a` + Singular Countable Noun + Singular Verb (*Many a student has failed*) |
