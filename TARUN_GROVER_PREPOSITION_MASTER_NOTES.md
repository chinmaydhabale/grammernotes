## 1. Preposition Definition, Etymology & Core Grammar Foundation

### 🔍 Preposition kya hota hai?
- **Etymology (शब्द की उत्पत्ति)**:
  $$\mathbf{PRE\ (पहले)\ +\ POSITION\ (स्थान)}$$
  Preposition ek aisa word hota hai jo kisi **Noun ya Pronoun ke pehle** baithkar sentence ke kisi doosre word (**Verb, Adjective, ya doosre Noun**) ke sath uska sambandh (relationship) sthapit karta hai.

---

### 👑 The Prepositional Object Rule (Cardinal Rule):
1. **Objective Case Mandatory**:
   Preposition ke baad aane wala Noun ya Pronoun uska **Object** kehlata hai, aur Pronoun hamesha **OBJECTIVE CASE** me hi hona chahiye:
   - Objective pronouns: `me, us, him, her, them, whom`.
   - *Error*: *Between you and I, there should be no secrets.* ❌  
     → *Correction*: *Between you and **me**, there should be no secrets.* ✅
   - *Error*: *She divided the property between he and she.* ❌  
     → *Correction*: *She divided the property between **him** and **her**.* ✅

2. **Gerund after Preposition**:
   Preposition ke baad agar koi Action (Verb) aati hai, to wo hamesha **Noun/Gerund (`V1 + ing`)** form me hogi:
   - *He is fond **of playing** cricket.* ✅ (NOT *of play* ❌).
   - *She is good **at dancing**.* ✅

## 2. Prepositions of Movement & Direction: "TO" vs. "TOWARDS"

```
                         ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
                         │             TO vs. TOWARDS             │
                         └───────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                             │
               ┌─────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────┐
               ▼                                                           ▼
     【 "TO" = Destination 】                                    【 "TOWARDS" = Direction 】
     • Manzil (Final Stop) nischit hoti hai.                     • Sirf rukh (Disha) batata hai;
     • Vahan pahunchna pakka hota hai.                           • Zaroori nahi ki manzil wahi ho.
     • *He is going TO the college.*                             • *He was walking TOWARDS the college.*
       (College uski manzil hai)                                   (College ki taraf ja raha tha, shayad
                                                                    kisi doosri dukaan par jana ho)
```

---

### 📌 Verbs taking "TO" for Destination:
Verbs of motion jaise: `Go, Come, Return, Travel, Send, March` apne sath destination jodne ke liye `TO` leti hain:
- *My father is going **to** the office.* ✅
- *She returned **to** her native village.* ✅

---

### ⚠️ The "SEND" Pattern Trap:
Examiners aksar `Send` ke do patterns par question banate hain:
1. **Pattern A (`Send + Person + Thing`)**:
   - Yahan koi preposition **nahi aati**:
   - *Please send me the file.* ✅ (NOT *send to me the file* ❌).
2. **Pattern B (`Send + Thing + TO + Person`)**:
   - Yahan person se pehle **`TO` aana 100% zaroori hai**:
   - *Please send the file **to** me.* ✅ (NOT *send the file me* ❌).

## 3. The Golden "HOME" Rule & Possessive Exception

Yeh concept exam ka **one of the highest frequency rules** hai:

```
                  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                  │                 THE "HOME" PREPOSITION RULE               │
                  └─────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┘
                                                │
                ┌───────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────┐
                ▼                                                               ▼
      【 CASE 1: HOME ALONE (No Preposition) 】                       【 CASE 2: POSSESSIVE + HOME (Use "TO") 】
      • Agar motion verb ke baad seedhe HOME aaye:                    • Agar HOME se pehle POSSESSIVE ADJECTIVE
        `go, come, return, arrive + HOME`                               (`my, your, his, her, their, our`) ya
      • Preposition "TO" KABHI NAHI LAGTA!                              Possessive Noun (`Ram's home`) aaye:
      • *I am going HOME.* ✅                                         • Preposition "TO" 100% MANDATORY hai!
      • *He returned HOME safely.* ✅                                 • *I am going TO MY HOME.* ✅
```

---

### 🔍 Deep Concept: Aisa kyu hota hai?
- **Grammar Logic**:
  - Jab *home* akele aata hai motion verb ke baad, to wo Noun nahi balki **ADVERB OF PLACE** ka kaam karta hai (Where? ka answer).
  - English grammar ka niyam hai: **Verb aur Adverb ke beech me Preposition KABHI NAHI aati**!
    - Jaise hum bolte hain: *She walked fast.* (NOT *walked with fast* ❌).
    - Waise hi: *I went home.* (NOT *went to home* ❌).
- **Lekin jab Possessive lagta hai**:
  - `My home`, `His home`, `Ram's home` me *home* **NOUN** ban jata hai (kyunki possessive adjective sirf Noun ko qualify karta hai).
  - Ab ye destination Noun hai, isliye **Preposition `TO` lagna 100% Compulsory** ho jata hai!

| Incorrect Sentence ❌ | Correct Sentence ✅ | Grammatical Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *I am not feeling well, I should go **to home**.* | *I am not feeling well, I should **go home**.* | *Home* is Adverb of Place; no 'to' |
| *He decided to return **to home** before dark.* | *He decided to **return home** before dark.* | Motion verb + home (no 'to') |
| *She is going **my home** for the holidays.* | *She is going **to my home** for the holidays.* | *My home* is Noun $
→$ requires 'to' |
| *Rohan went **his friend's home** yesterday.* | *Rohan went **to his friend's home** yesterday.* | Possessive Noun $
→$ requires 'to' |

## 4. Along vs. Along With & Across vs. Through

### 1. ALONG vs. ALONG WITH:
- **ALONG (किनारे-किनारे / समानांतर दिशा में)**:
  - Ek chhor se doosre chhor tak parallel line me chalna (*In a line parallel to the edge*):
  - *He was walking **along the road**.* (Road ke kinare-kinare).
  - *Trees are planted **along the river bank**.*
- **ALONG WITH (के साथ / संग)**:
  - Together with / In company of someone:
  - *He went to the party **along with his family**.* ✅
  - *The manager, **along with his team**, attended the seminar.* ✅

---

### 2. ACROSS vs. THROUGH:
```
                      ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
                      │           ACROSS vs. THROUGH           │
                      └───────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                          │
            ┌─────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────┐
            ▼                                                           ▼
  【 ACROSS (From one side to the other) 】                     【 THROUGH (Via 3D Enclosed Space) 】
  • Kisi satah (surface) ya rekha ke ek chhor se              • Kisi band / 3-Dimensional jagah ke
    doosre chhor tak:                                           andar dakhil hokar doosre chhor se bahar nikalna:
  • *He swam ACROSS the river.*                                • *The train passed THROUGH the tunnel.*
  • *She walked ACROSS the street.*                            • *We walked THROUGH the dense forest.*
  • Meaning: "On the opposite side"                            • *Light passed THROUGH the window.*
    (*My house is ACROSS the road*)
```

## 5. In vs. Into & On vs. Onto / Upon (Position vs. Motion)

Exam me **Static State (स्थिर अवस्था)** aur **Dynamic Motion (गतिशील अवस्था)** ka direct difference poocha jata hai:

| State | Enclosed Space (अंदर) | Surface (सतह पर) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **STATIC (Rest / No Motion)** | **IN** (*The students are **in** the room.*) | **ON** (*The book is **on** the table.*) |
| **DYNAMIC (Motion / Movement)** | **INTO** (*He jumped **into** the river.*) | **ONTO / UPON** (*The cat jumped **onto** the table.*) |

---

### 🔍 Key Nuances & Exceptions:
1. **INTO for Transformation (रूपांतरण)**:
   - Jab koi vastu ek avastha se doosri avastha me badalti hai:
   - *Translate this passage **into** Hindi.* ✅
   - *Water turns **into** ice at zero degrees.* ✅
2. **The "ENTER" Zero Preposition Trap**:
   - `Enter` ka matlab hi hota hai *go into / come into*.
   - Isliye physical jagah me ghusne ke liye `Enter` ke baad **`INTO` lagana STRICTLY PROHIBITED** hai:
     - *Error*: *The teacher **entered into** the classroom.* ❌  
       → *Correction*: *The teacher **entered** the classroom.* ✅
   - **Exception**: Kisi agreement, pact, treaty ya discussion me shamil hona:
     - *The two companies **entered into an agreement**.* ✅

## 6. The Height & Depth Matrix: Above vs. Below & Over vs. Under

Tarun Grover sir ka **"Level vs. Vertical Line" Master Matrix**:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                              HEIGHT & DEPTH MASTER DISTINCTION MATRIX                                  │
├─────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Category            │ UPWARD / HIGHER                     │ DOWNWARD / LOWER                           │
├─────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ **LEVEL / SCALE**   │ **ABOVE**                           │ **BELOW**                                  │
│ (Standard/Rank/Mark)│ • *Above the danger mark*           │ • *Below the poverty line*                 │
│                     │ • *Above 10,000 rupees*             │ • *Below my dignity (शान के खिलाफ)*       │
│                     │ • *Above sea level*                 │ • *Temperatures below freezing*            │
├─────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ **VERTICAL / COVER**│ **OVER**                            │ **UNDER**                                  │
│ (Directly Overhead /│ • *Fan is spinning OVER my head*     │ • *Sitting UNDER the tree*                 │
│  Covering / Motion) │ • *Spread blanket OVER the baby*    │ • *The cat is UNDER the table*             │
│                     │ • *Jumped OVER the hurdle*          │ • *Under age / Under construction*         │
└─────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

### 📌 Deep Rules for OVER vs. ABOVE:
1. **Vertical Alignment (लंबवत)**:
   - Agar koi cheez aapke theek upar (90 degree) hai bina touch kiye $
→$ **OVER**:
     - *There is a ceiling fan **over** my head.* ✅
2. **Covering Surface (ढकना)**:
   - Kisi cheez ko poori tarah chhadit karne ya dhakne ke liye hamesha **OVER** lagta hai:
     - *Put a cloth **over** the table.* ✅
     - *Spread the warm blanket **over** the sleeping baby.* ✅ (NOT *above the baby* ❌).
3. **Movement across an Obstacle (बाधा पार करना)**:
   - Kisi deewar ya fence ke upar se kood kar nikalna:
   - *The thief jumped **over** the boundary wall.* ✅
4. **Levels & Standards $
→$ ABOVE**:
   - *His performance was **above** expectations.* ✅
   - *Inflation is currently **above** the RBI's target.* ✅

---

### 📌 Deep Rules for UNDER vs. BELOW:
1. **Vertical Depth $
→$ UNDER**:
   - *The dog was resting **under** the shade of the mango tree.* ✅
   - *He hid the secret documents **under** the mattress.* ✅
2. **Standard, Level & Dignity $
→$ BELOW**:
   - *I will never do anything that is **below my dignity**.* ✅
   - *Those families are living **below** the official poverty line.* ✅
   - *His marks were **below** the cutoff score.* ✅
3. **Status & Condition $
→$ UNDER**:
   - *Under control, Under investigation, Under surveillance, Under age, Under repair*.

## 7. Between vs. Among vs. Amid / Amidst (The Uncountable Trap)

```
                            ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
                            │      BETWEEN vs. AMONG vs. AMID        │
                            └───────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                                │
         ┌──────────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────┐
         ▼                                      ▼                                      ▼
   【 BETWEEN 】                              【 AMONG 】                            【 AMID / AMIDST 】
   • Exactly **DO (2)** entities ke          • **2 se zyada COUNTABLE**              • **UNCOUNTABLE Nouns** ya
     beech bantwara ya tulna:                  entities ke beech me:                   bheed/avvyavastha ke beech:
   • *Between Ram and Shyam*                  • *Among the students*                  • *Amid the wreckage (मलबा)*
   • Exception: Mutually reciprocal           • *Among the poor people*               • *Amid the garbage (कूड़ा)*
     (*Treaty between three nations*)         • *Amongst us* (before vowels)          • *Amid chaotic scenes*
```

---

### 🔍 Exam Questions from Class:
1. *A child's doll was found _______ the wreckage of the crashed helicopter.*
   - Options: *between / among / amid*
   - **Reasoning**: Helicopter ka wreckage (मलबा) ek mass/uncountable debris hai, discrete countable units nahi.
   - **Correct Answer**: **AMID** the wreckage ✅
2. *A valuable diamond ring was found _______ the heap of garbage.*
   - **Reasoning**: Garbage (कूड़ा-कचरा) Uncountable noun hai $
→$ **AMID** the heap of garbage ✅.
3. *The property was divided _______ the four brothers.*
   - **Reasoning**: Countable plural noun $
→$ **AMONG** the four brothers ✅.

## 8. Prepositions of Place: "IN" vs. "AT" (The Relative Size Rule)

```
                        ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
                        │             IN vs. AT (Place)          │
                        └───────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                            │
              ┌─────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────┐
              ▼                                                           ▼
    【 "IN" = Large Areas / Enclosed 】                         【 "AT" = Specific Point / Smaller Area 】
    • Countries, Continents, States, Large Cities.              • Chhoti jagah, colony, gaon, specific point.
    • *In India, in Asia, in Haryana, in Delhi*                 • Badi jagah ke comparison me chhoti jagah.
    • Enclosed Space: *in the room, in the building*            • *At Bapu Nagar in Jaipur*
```

---

### 🎯 The Relative Hierarchy Rule:
Agar ek hi sentence me do places mention hon (ek chhota aur ek bada):
$$\mathbf{AT\ +\ Smaller\ Place\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ IN\ +\ Larger\ Area}$$

#### Examples:
- *He lives **at** Bapu Nagar **in** Jaipur.* ✅
- *She stays **at** Sonipat **in** Haryana.* ✅
- *I live **in** India.* ✅ (NOT *at India* ❌).
- *Address with exact house number*: *He lives **at** Flat 402, Green Apartments.* ✅

## 9. Prepositions of Time: "AT", "ON", "IN" (Parts of the Day Master Scale)

Tarun Grover sir ka **"Scale of Time" Formula**:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                              TIME PREPOSITIONS MASTER CONTINUUM                                        │
├──────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Preposition  │ Dimension / Nature                              │ Examples                              │
├──────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────┤
│ **AT**       │ **Exact Moments & Miniature Points of the Day** │ • `at 5:00 PM`, `at 10:30 AM`         │
│              │ (Bhor, dopahar, godhuli, raat ke nischit bindu) │ • `at dawn` (भोर / पहली किरण)         │
│              │                                                 │ • `at sunrise`, `at sunset`           │
│              │                                                 │ • `at noon`, `at midday`              │
│              │                                                 │ • `at dusk` (गोधूलि वेला)              │
│              │                                                 │ • `at night`, `at midnight`           │
├──────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────┤
│ **ON**       │ **Particular Days & Calendar Dates**            │ • `on Monday`, `on Sunday morning`    │
│              │ (24-hour cycle)                                 │ • `on 15th August`, `on 2nd October`  │
│              │                                                 │ • `on my birthday`, `on Diwali day`   │
├──────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────┤
│ **IN**       │ **Long Extended Intervals & Seasons**           │ • `in the morning`, `in the evening`  │
│              │ (Chhota moment nahi, lamba span)                │ • `in July`, `in summer`, `in winter` │
│              │                                                 │ • `in 2024`, `in the 21st century`    │
└──────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

### ⚠️ Night ke sath Special Distinction:
- **General Routine (रात में)** $
→$ **`AT night`**:
  - *Owls hunt **at night**.* ✅
- **Specific Particular Night (उस विशेष रात)** $
→$ **`IN the night`**:
  - *He woke up suddenly **in the night** due to the thunder.* ✅

## 10. Fixed Prepositions Masterclass

Tarun Grover sir ne class me sabse zyada pooche jane wale confusing fixed preposition groups ko context aur examples ke sath sikhaya hai:

---

### 1. DIE (मृत्यु होना)
| Fixed Pair | Trigger / Context | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Die OF** | **Bimari, Rog, Bhookh, Pyaas** (*Disease, Illness, Hunger, Thirst*) | • *He died **of** cholera / cancer / malaria.*<br>• *The poor man died **of** starvation.* |
| **Die FROM** | **Ghaav, Zahar, Overeating, Rakt-srav, Baahri kaaran** (*Wound, Overeating, External cause*) | • *He died **from** loss of blood.*<br>• *The patient died **from** food poisoning / overwork.* |
| **Die FOR** | **Desh, Mahan lakshya, Balidan** (*Noble Cause, Country*) | • *Bhagat Singh died **for** his motherland.* |
| **Die IN** | **Karyakal, Durghatna, Yuddh** (*Harness / Duty, Accident, Battle*) | • *The president died **in harness** (on duty).*<br>• *He died **in** a road accident.* |

---

### 2. BLIND (अंधा होना)
| Fixed Pair | Meaning & Context | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Blind IN** | **Ek aankh se andha hona** (*Left eye / Right eye / One eye*) | • *The old beggar was blind **in** the left eye.* |
| **Blind WITH** | **Dono aankhon se andha hona** (*Both eyes*) | • *He has been blind **with** both eyes since birth.* |
| **Blind TO** | **Galtiyon ya kamiyon ko nazarandaz karna** (*Faults, Mistakes, Defects*) | • *Parents should not be blind **to** the faults of their children.* |

---

### 3. ANXIOUS (चिंतित / उत्सुक)
- **Anxious FOR + Safety / Wellbeing / Result**: Kisi ki suraksha ya bhalayi ke liye chintit hona:
  - *My parents were **anxious for** my safety when it rained heavily.* ✅
- **Anxious ABOUT + Outcome / Event**: Kisi aane wale event ya result ko lekar ghabrahat hona:
  - *Students are **anxious about** the upcoming board examination.* ✅

---

### 4. BURST (फूट पड़ना / धावा बोलना)
- **Burst INTO + Tears / Laughter**: Achanak phoot-phoot kar rona ya hasi se lotpot hona:
  - *On hearing the tragic news, she **burst into tears**.* ✅
  - *The audience **burst into laughter**.* ✅
- **Burst UPON + Place / City**: Achanak hamla karna ya toot padna:
  - *The enemy troops **burst upon** the undefended village.* ✅

---

### 5. CARE (परवाह / देखभाल)
- **Take care OF**: Dhyan rakhna / Sambhalna:
  - *You must take good **care of** your health.* ✅
- **Care FOR**: Kisi cheez ko chahna / pasand karna / sewa karna:
  - *A true saint does not **care for** worldly wealth.* ✅
  - *She spent years caring **for** her ailing mother.* ✅

---

### 6. COMPLAIN (शिकायत करना)
| Fixed Form | Target | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Complain TO** | **Jisse shikayat ki jaye** (*Person in authority*) | • *The residents complained **to** the society secretary.* |
| **Complain AGAINST**| **Jiske khilaf shikayat ho** (*Accused person*) | • *They lodged a complaint against the security guard.* |
| **Complain OF / ABOUT**| **Jis samasya / ghatna ki shikayat ho** (*Thing / Problem*) | • *He complained **about** the recent theft.*<br>• *She complained **of** severe migraine.* |

---

### 7. AGREE (सहमत होना)
- **Agree WITH + Person**: Kisi insaan ke sath sehmat hona:
  - *I completely **agree with** you on this matter.* ✅
- **Agree TO + Proposal / Plan / View**: Kisi prastav ya sujhav par sehmat hona:
  - *The board members **agreed to** my expansion proposal.* ✅
- **Agree ON + Point / Terms**: Kisi nischit mudde ya shart par sabhi ka sehmat hona:
  - *They could not **agree on** the terms of the contract.* ✅

---

### 8. LEAN (सहारा लेना / झुकना)
- **Lean AGAINST + Wall / Surface**: Deewar ya physical surface se tik kar khada hona:
  - *Do not **lean against** the freshly painted car/wall.* ✅
- **Lean ON / UPON + Somebody for support**: Kisi par aashrit ya nirbhar hona:
  - *In difficult times, he **leans upon** his elder brother for guidance.* ✅
- **Lean TOWARDS + Idea / Philosophy**: Kisi vichardhara ya siddhant ki taraf jhukav hona:
  - *Many modern youth **lean towards** Gandhian simplicity.* ✅

---

### 9. LIVE (जीवन व्यतीत करना / जीना)
- **Live ON + Food / Diet**: Koi vishisht bhojan kha kar jeevit rehna:
  - *Cows **live on** grass.* ✅
  - *The ascetics **live on** fruits and raw vegetables.* ✅
- **Live BY + Principles / Labor**: Apni mehnat se rozi kamana ya siddhanton par jeena:
  - *He is an honest man who **lives by** hard labor.* ✅
  - *Those who **live by** the sword die by the sword.* ✅
- **Live FOR + Noble Cause**: Kisi mahan uddeshya ke liye apna jeevan samarpit karna:
  - *Mother Teresa **lived for** the service of the destitute.* ✅
- **Live WITHIN + Means**: Apni aamdani ke dayre me rehna:
  - *Proverb*: *Cut your coat according to your cloth; **live within** your means.* ✅

### 10. PART (अलग होना / जुदा होना)
Tarun Grover sir ka **Super Hit Exam Distinction**:

```
                         ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
                         │             PART WITH vs. FROM         │
                         └───────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                             │
               ┌─────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────┐
               ▼                                                           ▼
     【 PART WITH + SOMETHING 】                                 【 PART FROM + SOMEONE 】
     • Kisi vastu, dhan ya sampatti ko chhodna                   • Kisi vyakti / parivar se alag hona
       ya usse juda hona:                                          ya juda hona:
     • *A miser cannot PART WITH his money.*                     • *I can part with my wealth, but I*
     • *She refused to PART WITH her heirloom.*                    *cannot PART FROM my parents.*
```

---

### 11. PROCEED (आगे बढ़ना)
- **Proceed WITH + Work / Discussion**: Kisi shuru kiye hue kaam ko aage badhana:
  - *Let us **proceed with** today's agenda.* ✅
- **Proceed FROM + Source**: Kisi srot ya karan se nikalna:
  - *His deep confidence **proceeds from** years of rigorous practice.* ✅
- **Proceed ON / UPON + Beliefs / Principles**: Apne vishwas ya siddhant ke aadhar par aage badhna:
  - *Every individual should **proceed on** what they genuinely believe.* ✅

## 11. Omission of Preposition Traps

Examiner aksar un verbs ke baad preposition laga kar error deta hai jinke andar preposition ka arth pehle se samahit (inherent) hota hai:

| Verb | Meaning | Common Error ❌ | Correct Form ✅ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Discuss** | Talk about | *Let us discuss **about** the problem.* ❌ | *Let us **discuss the problem**.* ✅ |
| **Describe** | Tell about | *She described **about** the accident.* ❌ | *She **described the accident**.* ✅ |
| **Order** | Request for | *He ordered **for** three cups of tea.* ❌ | *He **ordered three cups** of tea.* ✅ |
| **Enter** | Go into | *The students entered **into** the hall.* ❌ | *The students **entered the hall**.* ✅ |
| **Attack** | Strike at | *The army attacked **on** the enemy base.* ❌ | *The army **attacked the enemy base**.* ✅ |
| **Accompany** | Go with | *I will accompany **with** you.* ❌ | *I will **accompany you**.* ✅ |
| **Resemble** | Similar to | *She resembles **to / with** her mother.* ❌ | *She **resembles her mother**.* ✅ |
| **Comprise** | Consist of | *The book comprises **of** ten chapters.* ❌ | *The book **comprises ten chapters**.* ✅ |

## 12. Master Practice Question Bank

Tarun Grover sir ke masterclass ke sabhi class questions ka detailed Hindi reasoning ke sath solution:

---

### 📝 Question 1:
**Question**: *I am not feeling well today, (A) / so I have decided (B) / to go to home early. (C) / No Error (D)*
- **Error**: Part (C)
- **Explanation**: Motion verb `go` ke baad jab `home` akele aata hai, to wo Adverb of Place hota hai. Verb aur Adverb ke beech Preposition nahi aati $
→$ **"to go home early"**.

---

### 📝 Question 2:
**Question**: *She decided to return (A) / back to her home (B) / before sunset. (C) / No Error (D)*
- **Error**: Part (B)
- **Explanation**: `Return` ke sath `back` superfluous hai, aur *her home* se pehle `to` theek hai lekin `back` hatana padega $
→$ **"to return to her home"**.

---

### 📝 Question 3:
**Question**: *Please send (A) / the official files me (B) / as soon as possible. (C) / No Error (D)*
- **Error**: Part (B)
- **Explanation**: Pattern hai `Send + Thing + TO + Person` $
→$ **"the official files to me"**.

---

### 📝 Question 4:
**Question**: *He was walking (A) / along with the railway track (B) / listening to music. (C) / No Error (D)*
- **Error**: Part (B)
- **Explanation**: Railway track ke kinare parallel chalne ke liye `along` lagta hai (`along with` company ke liye aata hai) $
→$ **"along the railway track"**.

---

### 📝 Question 5:
**Question**: *A gold necklace was discovered (A) / among the heap of debris (B) / in the old mansion. (C) / No Error (D)*
- **Error**: Part (B)
- **Explanation**: Heap of debris (मलबा) uncountable mass hai, isliye `among` nahi balki `amid` aayega $
→$ **"amid the heap of debris"**.

---

### 📝 Question 6:
**Question**: *The baby was crying, (A) / so the mother spread (B) / a warm blanket above him. (C) / No Error (D)*
- **Error**: Part (C)
- **Explanation**: Kisi cheez ko poori tarah cover karne ya dhakne ke liye `over` lagta hai (`above` nahi) $
→$ **"blanket over him"**.

---

### 📝 Question 7:
**Question**: *Taking bribes is completely (A) / under my dignity, (B) / so I rejected the offer. (C) / No Error (D)*
- **Error**: Part (B)
- **Explanation**: Dignity, rank ya standard ke liye `below` lagta hai $
→$ **"below my dignity"**.

---

### 📝 Question 8:
**Question**: *The patient died (A) / from cancer after battling (B) / the illness for two years. (C) / No Error (D)*
- **Error**: Part (B)
- **Explanation**: Rog ya bimari se maut hone par `Die OF` lagta hai (`from` external causes ke liye hota hai) $
→$ **"died of cancer"**.

---

### 📝 Question 9:
**Question**: *He has been blind (A) / with the right eye (B) / since childhood. (C) / No Error (D)*
- **Error**: Part (B)
- **Explanation**: Kisi ek aankh se andha hone par `Blind IN` lagta hai (`with` dono aankhon ke liye aata hai) $
→$ **"blind in the right eye"**.

---

### 📝 Question 10:
**Question**: *The affectionate father (A) / is completely blind (B) / in the faults of his son. (C) / No Error (D)*
- **Error**: Part (C)
- **Explanation**: Galtiyon ya doshon par aankhein moondne ke liye `Blind TO` lagta hai $
→$ **"blind to the faults of his son"**.

---

### 📝 Question 11:
**Question**: *The miserly merchant (A) / could never part from (B) / even a single penny of his wealth. (C) / No Error (D)*
- **Error**: Part (B)
- **Explanation**: Dhan ya vastu se alag hone ke liye `Part WITH` lagta hai (`part from` insaan ke liye hota hai) $
→$ **"part with even a single penny"**.

---

### 📝 Question 12:
**Question**: *The students entered (A) / into the examination hall (B) / precisely at 9:00 AM. (C) / No Error (D)*
- **Error**: Part (B)
- **Explanation**: Physical space me dakhil hone ke liye `Enter` ke sath `into` nahi lagta $
→$ **"entered the examination hall"**.

## 13. Quick Revision Summary (Exam Cheat Sheet)

| Concept / Category | Quick Formula |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Prepositional Object** | Preposition + **Objective Case Pronoun** (*between you and me*) |
| **Motion to Home** | `go / come / return + HOME` (NO 'TO') \| `go + TO + my/his/Ram's home` |
| **Parallel Movement** | **Along** the road/track \| **Along with** my friends |
| **Enclosed Motion** | Rest = **IN / ON** \| Motion = **INTO / ONTO** |
| **Above vs. Below** | Standards, Levels, Sea level, Cutoff, Dignity |
| **Over vs. Under** | Vertical line (90°), Covering blanket over baby, jumping over hurdle |
| **Between / Among / Amid** | 2 entities = **Between** \| >2 Countable = **Among** \| Uncountable/Chaos = **Amid** |
| **Parts of Day Time** | Moments = **AT** (dawn, noon, dusk, night) \| Periods = **IN** (morning, evening) |
| **Die Prepositions** | **Die OF** disease/hunger \| **Die FROM** wound/overwork \| **Die FOR** country |
| **Blind Prepositions** | **Blind IN** one eye \| **Blind WITH** both eyes \| **Blind TO** faults |
| **Part Prepositions** | **Part WITH** money/things \| **Part FROM** parents/people |
| **Lean Prepositions** | **Lean AGAINST** wall \| **Lean UPON** brother \| **Lean TOWARDS** ideology |
| **Zero Preposition** | *Discuss, Describe, Order, Attack, Enter (room), Accompany* $
→$ No Preposition |
